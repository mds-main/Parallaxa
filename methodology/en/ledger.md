# Ledger, briefing and corrections

## Actor Ledger

The Ledger records what public actors (politicians, parties, governments, public figures, influencers with large reach, companies, media outlets) actually said and did, using one rubric for everyone:

- factual reliability (no score until at least 10 claims have been analyzed);
- correction behaviour; data integrity; prediction calibration;
- integrity record (final court rulings, audits and regulator sanctions only, never allegations);
- links to influence operations (with source and confidence);
- civility of conduct.

There is no single good-or-bad score. We describe conduct, never character. Every actor can reply, and replies are published next to the record. Decisions can be appealed, first to a different reviewer and model pair, then to the cross-partisan panel.

## Daily Parallax

Each edition (Global, each country, each covered region) has 5–10 candidates chosen by published rules: how prominent a story is across outlets on both sides, how viral its contested claims are, and upcoming decisions. People review every edition before 07:00 local time. Your briefing is then assembled on your device from your places and topics, with one optional story from outside your filters.

## Corrections

Analyses are never edited silently. A correction creates a new version with a public diff and the reason. Validated errors are corrected within 72 hours, and every correction is listed in the corrections log and its RSS feed.
