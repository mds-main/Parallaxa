# Sources

Investigations look for primary sources first.

| Tier | What it includes |
|---|---|
| 1. Primary | Official statistics (INE, Eurostat, BLS, Census, OECD, WHO), court rulings, legislation, full transcripts and recordings, peer-reviewed research. |
| 2. Research and wires | Systematic reviews, reputable research institutes, wire services. |
| 3. News outlets | Established outlets across the political spectrum. |
| 4. Context only | Everything else, including posts on X and other social networks. Never enough for a grade on its own. |

Rules:

- A claim can only be graded supported or contradicted with at least two independent sources, including one from tier 1 or 2.
- Tiers are assigned from the source's domain, never from what a model claims.
- Every quote is checked word for word against the page. Unverified quotes are removed.
- Pages are fetched with protections against hidden instructions: scripts, comments and hidden text are stripped, and nothing in a page can change our instructions.
- Evidence older than 12 months is flagged.
