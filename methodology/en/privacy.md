# Privacy

*Summary of how Parallaxa handles data. The full legal privacy policy will be published after legal review.*

- **No account needed.** Checks, the briefing and the quiz work anonymously. Your history and preferences stay on your device.
- **No ads, no tracking.** We use no third-party analytics or advertising scripts. Usage figures are anonymous counters with no identifiers.
- **IP addresses** are kept only as daily-salted hashes for rate limiting, for at most 7 days.
- **What you paste** is sent to AI models that do not retain data ("private" models). Models that could see prompts only ever receive a cleaned-up version of the claim with personal data removed, plus public sources.
- **Places and topics** you choose can reveal political interests, so they stay on your device unless you create an account, and then they are encrypted.
- **Political self-placement** is optional, needs your explicit consent, is stored separately and encrypted, and is only used in large anonymous aggregates.
- Data is stored in the EU. You can download or delete everything at any time from Your Parallaxa.
