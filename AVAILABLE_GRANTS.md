# Available grants, credits and support programs

Researched on **23 November 2026**, from official pages wherever possible. Programs change often: check the linked page before applying. Items marked *(2nd-hand)* come from an aggregator or a national contact point and must be confirmed on the official portal (for EU calls: the [Funding & Tenders portal](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/)).

Parallaxa's profile for every application: open source (AGPL), non-profit, ad-free, based in Spain (EU), English and Spanish, countering misinformation and polarisation with several AI models from different countries, every source shown. A digital public good (SDG 16).

## The short version

- **No program pays for production AI inference at scale.** The realistic mix is: a legal identity, open-source infrastructure sponsorships, a few AI and tool credits, a fellowship, and EU consortium grants for real money.
- **A legal identity unlocks most of it.** Nearly every nonprofit offer, Spanish grant and EU call needs a registered entity: a Spanish *asociación* or *fundación*, or a fiscal host such as Open Source Europe ASBL. Nonprofit discounts (Microsoft, Google, Anthropic, OpenAI, Resend, GitHub) are validated through **Goodstack** or a government register.
- **Startup programs usually exclude nonprofits** (Google for Startups, Microsoft for Startups, Cloudflare for Startups). The search-API startup programs below are the exception worth trying: some accept bootstrapped teams.
- **Stay non-partisan in every application.** Microsoft, GitHub and Resend exclude political organisations, and Open Source Europe forbids political campaigning. Parallaxa is non-partisan by design; say so explicitly.

## 5. What to prepare once, reuse everywhere

- **One-page summary** in English and Spanish: problem, approach (several models from different countries, published combining rule, every source shown), what is open, privacy stance, non-partisanship.
- **Evidence**: live demo link, public eval results and per-edition audit (already in the app), usage and accuracy numbers once launched.
- **Governance and policies** (DPG and most funders ask): licence, privacy policy, content moderation and do-no-harm statement, conflict-of-interest rules for models, corrections ledger.
- **Budget**: AI inference per analysis (shown in Admin › Models), search and tool costs, hosting, moderation and editorial staff.
- **Partners** for consortium calls: Spanish universities (IBERIFIER members), IFCN/EFCSN fact-checkers, a media-literacy NGO in two other EU countries.
